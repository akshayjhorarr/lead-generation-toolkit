# SaaS Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Industry
SaaS

## Company Size
10-200 employees

## Revenue Range
$1M - $10M ARR

## Target Roles
- Founder
- Head of Sales
- Revenue Operations Manager

## Common Pain Points
- Low outbound conversion
- Poor follow-up systems
- Unorganized lead management
- Limited SDR automation

## Goals
- Increase booked meetings
- Improve reply rates
- Scale outbound outreach
